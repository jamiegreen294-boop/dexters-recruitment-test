Recruitment Privacy Notice — acknowledgement

Dexter's uses recruitment information to administer your application, assess suitability, complete lawful pre-employment checks, issue employment documents and create your staff record if appointed. Access is limited to authorised people with a genuine business need.

Successful-candidate information required for employment will transfer into the staff record. Recruitment-only information will be retained and deleted under Dexter's approved retention arrangements. Sensitive information is restricted and handled separately where required.

This acknowledgement records that the notice has been provided; it is not used as general consent for all employment data processing.
