DEXTER'SRECRUITMENT

Candidate Recruitment File

This pack is designed to create one auditable recruitment record from application through to employment.

Required file checks

Application completed and assessed against the role criteria.
Interview / selection record completed.
Right to Work check completed before employment starts.
References / other lawful pre-employment checks completed where required.
Conditional offer and employment documentation issued.

Job Application Form

The selection decision should be based on role-related criteria, not nationality or another protected characteristic.

Fair & Equal Recruitment Checklist

Use the same essential criteria and selection method for candidates competing for the same role.

Job description and person specification are role-related and non-discriminatory.
Reasonable adjustments have been offered where required.
No applicant is rejected simply because they are non-British; the relevant question is whether they have the right to do the work offered.
Interview questions relate to competence, availability and genuine job requirements.
Selection reasons are documented.

Interview & Selection Record

Right to Work Check Record

Complete before employment starts. The same right-to-work requirement applies regardless of a candidate’s race or nationality.
I verified the person presenting the evidence is the person being recruited and recorded the check in accordance with current Home Office requirements.

Overseas / Non-British Applicant Record

This section is for lawful immigration/right-to-work administration. It must not be used to make assumptions about an applicant because of nationality.

Hours, job type and any restrictions were checked against the actual Dexter’s role offered.
Where status is time-limited, a lawful follow-up check has been scheduled.
No passport was retained beyond what is necessary; only appropriate evidence/records are stored securely.

Pre-employment Checks

Identity and contact details verified as appropriate.
Right to Work completed.
References requested where required by Dexter’s policy.
Role-specific qualifications checked where relevant.
Disclosure Scotland / PVG check only where the role is legally eligible or requires it.

Conditional Offer Record

Candidate told the offer is conditional until specified checks are satisfied.

New Starter Compliance Checklist

Sick pay/procedure, other paid leave and notice information made available.

Recruitment Privacy Notice — Test Structure

Dexter’s should tell applicants what personal information is collected, why it is used, the lawful basis, who receives it, how long it is kept, and the applicant’s data-protection rights.

Information commonly processed

Identity/contact details, application information, employment history, interview notes, references, right-to-work evidence and any lawful role-specific checks.

Special category / sensitive information

Only collect where necessary and lawful. Equality monitoring, if used, should be separated from selection decisions wherever practicable.

Retention

The final pack will include Dexter’s approved recruitment-retention schedule rather than keeping unsuccessful candidate records indefinitely.

Applicant has been given access to the recruitment privacy notice.
