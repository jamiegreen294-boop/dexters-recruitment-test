Recruitment Privacy & UK GDPR Record

This section records that the applicant has been told how Dexter's will use recruitment data and that staff have followed the approved data-handling process.

Recruitment privacy notice

Applicant was told the purposes for which recruitment data is used.
Applicant was told the categories of information processed, who may receive it and how long it may be retained.
Applicant was told how to exercise data-protection rights and how to contact Dexter's about their information.

Categories of recruitment information

Identity and contact information.
Application, employment history, education, skills and role-related responses.
Screening, assessment, interview scores and recruiter notes.
References and lawful pre-employment verification.
Right-to-work evidence and follow-up records where required by law.

Special category / higher-risk information

Do not collect this by default. Health/disability, ethnicity, religion, sexual orientation and other special-category information require a specific lawful reason and additional safeguards. Equality-monitoring information should be separated from the selection record and not shown to decision-makers.
No unnecessary special-category data has been added to the candidate file.
Any recruitment-adjustment information is restricted to those who need it to arrange the adjustment.
Equality monitoring, if used, is held separately and is not used to make the hiring decision.

Access, sharing and retention

Recruitment data will not be retained indefinitely.
Access to recruitment information is limited to people with a genuine business need.
