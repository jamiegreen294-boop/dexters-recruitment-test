Must add before the full connected test

1. One joined-up record. The full recruitment pack, public application, Back Office and candidate portal are currently separate test pages. The full test must use one candidate record so every answer, score, check, document and signature flows forward automatically.
2. Secure candidate access. Email invite/login or one-time secure link, expiry, logout, password/reset flow where applicable and separation between candidates.
3. Real communication service. Application acknowledgement, interview invite, rejection, offer/signing request, reminder and appointment emails need queued delivery plus sent/failed status in the audit trail.
4. Exact signed-document snapshots. At signing, preserve the exact document version the candidate saw, their signature, timestamp and audit identity. Later edits must create a new version rather than changing the signed copy.
5. Final employment documents. Add the formal employment contract / written particulars pack after conditions are satisfied, then sign it through the same portal and attach it to Staff.
6. Right-to-Work workflow. Support the appropriate Home Office/manual/digital route, record the check correctly and schedule follow-up checks for time-limited permission.
7. Interview recording decision. Decide whether Dexter's needs the audio itself or only an agreed live transcript. If audio is retained, add secure storage, access control and a defined retention rule.
8. Automated-score governance. Store suggested score, manager-confirmed score and override reason separately. Essential criteria can block progression, but a manager must confirm the employment decision.
9. Recruitment retention. Add retention/review dates for unsuccessful candidates and a clear handover rule defining which successful-candidate information moves into the staff file.
10. Vacancy management. Applications should belong to a named vacancy with opening/closing dates, number of positions, status and the exact job description/person specification version used for that recruitment round.

Recommended before launch

Interview scheduling/rescheduling and no-show/withdrawn statuses; second-reviewer/manager approval; reference reminders; offer-expiry reminders; candidate document download/print; accessibility/mobile checks; dashboard search/filter; overdue-action alerts; under-18/apprentice-specific checks; and a full audit log covering create/view/change/send/sign/approve/convert-to-staff actions.

Pre-build conclusion: The overall workflow is sound. The biggest job before the full test is not adding more forms — it is joining the existing forms, interview, offer, portal, signatures and staff handover into one secure candidate record and preserving the exact documents that were issued and signed.
